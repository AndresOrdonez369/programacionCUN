## Direction contract
THESIS: The course is one sprite sheet: Módulo 0 and sessions 1–8 are numbered frames 00–08 of a single animation. Refuses the LMS sidebar-and-cards default and the neon-arcade costume.
OWN-WORLD: Closed indexed palette of six inks, each shown with its index: 0 ink #1B1A2E, 1 paper #F4F1E8, 2 coral #FF5D3A, 3 teal #3BC4A3, 4 amber #FFC83D, 5 blue #5B6CFF. Dark ink masthead on an 8px pixel grid; paper reading ground. Silkscreen pixel face only for frame numbers, indices and small labels; Bricolage Grotesque for display; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading; JetBrains Mono for code. Square corners, 2px ink rules, hard offset shadows in palette inks. Raise (quarterly): nothing outside the six inks. Raise (plakatstil): motion by hard cuts / steps(), never fades; grids reflow in whole cells. Raise (cutting bench): state is a mark not a hue: seen frame = teal pixel, current = coral cell, "aquí te quedaste" persisted.
STORY: The student sees where the course stands, opens this session's four moves (pre-quiz, slides, code, post-quiz), studies alone, sees their gain.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Ink masthead: small pixel brand + nav, display title, one-line lede, indexed palette strip, then a full-width frame strip 00–08 (current frame coral). Below on paper: session title + question at left, four move cards with offset shadows, right aside with frame number, corte and analogy.
